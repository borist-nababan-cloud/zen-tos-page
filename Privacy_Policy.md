# Privacy Policy for Zen Internal App

**Last Updated: October 20, 2026**

Zen Family Spa ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose your information when you use the Zen Internal App.

### 1. Information We Collect
When you log in using Google OAuth, we collect certain information provided by Google, including:
*   **Name:** To identify you within the application.
*   **Email Address:** Used as your unique account identifier and for system notifications.
*   **Profile Picture (Avatar):** To personalize your user profile within the app.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:
*   To facilitate secure authentication and login.
*   To assign appropriate access roles (Operational Staff, Manager, etc.) based on your identity.
*   To support internal business operations, such as attendance and warehouse management.

### 3. Data Storage and Security
Your data is securely stored using **Supabase (PostgreSQL)** cloud infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

### 4. Third-Party Services
We use Google OAuth for authentication. Your use of Google services is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell or share your personal data with any other third parties.

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain your information as long as your internal account is active. If you wish to have your data deleted or your account deactivated, please contact our IT Department at **support@zenfamilyspa.id**.

### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
