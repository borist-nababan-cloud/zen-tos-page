Privacy Policy for Zen Internal App

Last Updated: October 20, 2026 • Published by: Zen Family Spa HQ

Zen Family Spa ("we," "our," or "us") is deeply committed to protecting the privacy and security of our personnel. This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which we collect, use, maintain, and disclose information collected from users (employees, therapists, managers, and authorized contractors) of the Zen Internal App.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in to the Zen Internal App using Google OAuth 2.0, we collect only the necessary identity data provided through your consent:

  • Full Name: To accurately identify you within the operational shift roster, attendance register, and task dispatch logs.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier, for authentication handshakes, and for critical operational notifications.
  • Profile Picture (Avatar URL): Used solely to render your staff avatar in the top navigation header and staff profile view.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is utilized strictly for authorized internal business operations:

  • To facilitate secure single-sign-on (SSO) authentication and active session maintenance.
  • To automatically provision and enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), mapping personnel to Role 12 (OPERATIONAL_STAFF) or administrative levels.
  • To synchronize work logs, biometric attendance records, room occupancy management, and back-of-house warehouse stock requests.

3. Data Storage, Hosting, and Security

Your profile and authentication metadata are securely stored using Supabase (PostgreSQL) enterprise cloud database infrastructure. We implement defensive security measures including:

  • PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies restricting record access exclusively to authorized callers.
  • End-to-end TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Strict audit logging and automated session token refresh cycles.

4. Third-Party Services & Zero Data Sale

We utilize Google OAuth strictly for user authentication. Your interaction with Google services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Absolute Protection Commitment: Zen Family Spa never sells, rents, monetizes, or trades employee personal data with external third parties, brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We retain account data for the duration of the staff member's active employment. Upon resignation or termination of duties, access is revoked immediately. To request account deletion or data anonymization, contact our IT Department at support@zenfamilyspa.id.

6. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect operational or regulatory improvements. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document.
