# zen-tos-page

Official Privacy Policy & Terms of Service legal compliance website for **Zen Family Spa & Reflexology** (`https://tos.zenfamilyspa.id`).

Designed for **Google OAuth 2.0 verification** and public compliance disclosures for the **Zen Internal Super-App**.

## 📄 Pages Included
* `index.html`: Unified compliance hub with executive Google API reviewer disclosures, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service.
* `privacy-policy.html`: Standalone direct route (`https://tos.zenfamilyspa.id/privacy-policy` or `/privacy-policy.html`).
* `terms-of-service.html`: Standalone direct route (`https://tos.zenfamilyspa.id/terms-of-service` or `/terms-of-service.html`).

## 🚀 Deployment
Ready for 1-click deployment on Coolify using the included `Dockerfile` and `nginx.conf`.
